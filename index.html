About

I like problems where the constraint is physical.

Heat, tolerance, cost, the width of a hand. I studied product design at MS Ramaiah University and have been at Exponent Energy since 2023, working across EV charging and battery hardware.

Most of what I do sits in the gap between what engineering says is possible and what a person will tolerate at 6am in a parking lot. I sketch a lot, build rough physical models early, and would rather test a bad prototype on Tuesday than a good render on Friday.
